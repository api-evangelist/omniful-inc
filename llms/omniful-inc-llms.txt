# Omniful, Inc.

> Omniful is an AI-powered unified supply chain and fulfillment platform (OMS, WMS, TMS, inventory, POS, returns) with public Integration APIs. Bearer-token authenticated, webhook-driven, base host prodapi.omniful.com. A 500 Global portfolio company operating primarily across the MENA region.

## APIs

- [Omniful Integration APIs (docs)](https://docs.omniful.tech): Orders, SKUs/catalog, inventory, hubs, returns, purchase orders, POS, customers, stock transfers, shipments/TMS, billing, reports, and webhooks. Base URL https://prodapi.omniful.com.
